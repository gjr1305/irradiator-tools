XRad225IR irradiator tools

Open tools from our TG-61 characterisation of the XRad225IR cabinet irradiator. Geofrey John, Adelaide University.

Dose and irradiation time calculator

Gives the irradiation time for a target dose. It uses our measured values for this machine: dose rate at the cells, backscatter, the 1.94 s ramp-up correction and timer linearity. We use it for all our cell irradiations.

Irradiator commissioning wizard

For other labs. It walks you through the measurements step by step, takes your own data, and builds a calculator for your machine at the end.

Open wizard →

Cabinet irradiator trainer

A browser simulator for new users, with a 3D cabinet and a console that works like the real one: key switch, door interlock, warm-up and filter check. Three practice scenarios (a 2 Gy exposure on a 6-well plate, spotting mistakes in a set-up, measuring the first HVL) and a dose explorer for distance, copper filtration, ramp-up and dose mode. It runs on our measured beam data, but it is for teaching only, not for working out real doses.
