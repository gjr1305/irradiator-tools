XRad225IR irradiator tools

Tools from a TG-61 characterisation of the XRad225IR cabinet irradiator. Geofrey John, Adelaide University.

Dose and irradiation time calculator

Gives the irradiation time for a target dose, using values measured for this machine: dose rate, backscatter, ramp-up correction and timer linearity.

Irradiator commissioning wizard

For other labs. Walks you through the measurements and builds a calculator for your own machine.

Cabinet irradiator trainer

A browser simulator for teaching new users, with a 3D cabinet, console, practice scenarios and a dose explorer.

For teaching only, not for working out real doses.

Send feedback

Found a bug, have a question or an idea? Your message goes straight to Geofrey.

Feedback is used only to improve these tools.
